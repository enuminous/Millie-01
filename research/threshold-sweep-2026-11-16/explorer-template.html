What changes the answer?

All six strict orderings occur when thresholds vary independently. On the common-multiplier diagonal, CUSUM is first or tied at 36 settings; all three miss at the highest setting. No monitor alerts on the nine other built-in noiseless scenarios at any tested threshold. Those controls do not establish a real-world false-alarm rate.

At the default CUSUM threshold, signed EWMA is faster when its threshold is below approximately 0.003546; it ties from there to 0.00523962 (upper endpoint excluded), and is later or undetected above that band. Exact floating-point boundaries are in the CSV files.
