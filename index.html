    What counts as evidence here?

    The EFMW-style monitor is the repository's ordinary signed EWMA: Mₜ = 0.97 Mₜ₋₁ + 0.03 rₜ. The matched signed-EWMA control must tie exactly. Absolute EWMA and two-sided CUSUM use the same input trace and separate calibration. Early alarms count against detection; misses receive the full observation-window delay penalty. Nothing in this bench establishes an EFMW-specific advantage or confirms physical safety.

    The model, honestly

    A real-time 3D heightfield with a ground-constrained six-wheel rover. Acceleration, turning inertia, traction, grade response, battery consumption, circular obstacle contacts, and wheel-height suspension are simplified models. There is no rigid-body solver, wheel lift, roll-over, soil deformation, or hardware connection.

    The larger outdoor research rover uses simulated 360° obstacle sensing and speeds up to 6 m/s. It is a separate virtual test envelope from the repository's low-speed indoor physical design.

    Autonomy uses a known map and A* routing, not SLAM or learned perception. Live monitor alerts cannot arm, drive, or stop the rover. Safety gates are a separate simulated state machine.
